> **💡 The TruthDraft Core Philosophy:**
> Algorithmic detectors calculate token probability and sentence burstiness—they **cannot** prove human or synthetic authorship. TruthDraft is built on an **"estimates, not proof"** foundation: providing transparent metrics for conscientious writers, researchers, and students to inspect stylistic flags and restore authentic human cadence without risking data privacy.
